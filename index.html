        👨‍👩‍👧 For parents and teachers

        A short guide for grown-ups.

        What is this?

        A playful maths world for Grade 2 learners (about ages 6–7). Topics are organised around the four strands of the Cambridge Primary Mathematics Stage 2 (0096) curriculum framework.

        • 🔢 Number
        • 📐 Geometry and Measure
        • 📊 Statistics and Probability
        • 🧠 Thinking and Working Mathematically — built into every place

        How each place will work

        Every learning place follows the same six steps, with hands-on activities rather than quizzes.

        1. 🌱 Learn
        2. 🎮 Play
        3. 🧩 Practise
        4. 🧠 Think
        5. 🚀 Challenge
        6. 🏆 Master

        Saved progress

        Stars, gems, badges and visited places are saved only in this browser on this device. There are no accounts and no ads.
